# Illustration provenance

`mascot.svg` and `favicon.svg` are original SVG marks authored for LocalAcademy
v0.3: a friendly green owl with a blue progress bar, drawn from simple shapes.
They are not Duolingo/Duo brand assets and must not be presented as such.

Font binaries are from `@fontsource/nunito` 5.3.0 (Nunito, SIL Open Font
License, included as `static/fonts/nunito-LICENSE.txt`). No remote assets.
